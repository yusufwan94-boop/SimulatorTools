How Image Formation Works

This simulator represents mirror optical mechanics. In school syllabi (both KSSM Malaysia and universal physics curriculums), mirror formulas dictate exactly where images are created:

Mirror Formula / Formula Cermin:
1/f = 1/u + 1/v
Magnification / Pembesaran Linear:
m = |v / u| = |h_i / h_o|

Light Ray Reference Rules:

  • Parallel Ray: Travels parallel to the principal axis, reflecting directly through the focal point (F).
  • Focal Ray: Travels directly through the focal point (F) and reflects parallel to the principal axis.
  • Radial Ray: Passes through the Center of Curvature (C) and reflects directly back along its original path.
